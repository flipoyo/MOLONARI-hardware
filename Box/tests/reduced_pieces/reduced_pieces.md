## Watertightness test box

### Purpose

Before printing the complete box, we wanted to validate the silicone seal on a smaller part that is quicker to print. This scaled-down box uses the same closing principle as the real box: a groove that holds the seal, and clamping with screws and nuts. If it stays watertight, the seal can be used on the complete box.

### Fabrication

1. **Print the half-box twice**: the same part is used for the top and the bottom, you just turn one of them over.
2. **Print the seal mold.**
3. **Cast the seal**, following the procedure described in [fabrication](../fabrication/README.md): mixing the two silicones, adding the activator, casting, 24 h of curing, then demolding.
4. **Insert the nuts** into their housings.

### Running the test

1. Place a **dry tissue** inside the box.
2. Lay the seal in the groove, without twisting or stretching it.
3. Close it with the second half-box and **tighten the screws with a power drill**, in a crisscross pattern.
4. Fully submerge the box in a tank of water, adding weight if needed.
5. Wait **at least 6 h**.
6. Take the box out, **dry it completely**, then open it and check the tissue:
   - dry tissue: the seal is validated;
   - damp tissue: check the condition of the seal (bubbles, holes) and the tightening, then try again.

### Result

The box passed the test easily. This is what convinced us to use cast silicone seals for the whole box.

## Probe seal

This part tests the watertightness of the passages through which the temperature probes enter the box. Each passage is made of two half-holes that close around the cable, and it is made watertight by a seal placed in the cylinder that extends the hole.

To test it, simply insert the end of the probe into this part and check that the whole assembly is watertight.

## Central part of the box

This is the part that sits in the middle of the box: the sensor is placed between its two halves. The idea was to put a foam seal on each side and drill holes so the two halves could be screwed together.

### Running the test

We placed the sensor in the middle, closed the part, then poured water on each side and checked that the sensor stayed dry.

### Result

This test was very difficult to carry out and not really conclusive. We later modified this part directly in the box.
