This guide explains how to install the sensor in the river, starting from the carrying case, and how to retrieve it afterwards.

## 1. Case contents

Check that everything is there **before you leave**:

| ✔ | Item | Quantity |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | Unassembled box (upper electronics section + lower sensor section) | 3 |
| ☐ | Weight holder | 1 |
| ☐ | 2 kg weights | 2 |
| ☐ | Rod with temperature probes to clip on | 1 |
| ☐ | Spare M5 screws | 10 |
| ☐ | Spare M5 nuts | 10 |
| ☐ | Silicone seals | 3 |
| ☐ | Screwing tool (Allen key / bit) | 1 |
| ☐ | Plugs (side cavities and calibration holes) | [TO BE COMPLETED] |
| ☐ | New spare batteries | 6 |
| ☐ | Printed deployment sheet + pencil | 1 |

**Also bring (not in the case):**
- crowbar and sledgehammer;
- charged power drill;
- boots or waders;
- phone (photos, GPS coordinates).

## 2. Before you leave

To be done **the day before or the same morning**, indoors:

1. **Batteries**: install new batteries.
2. **Function test**: check that the temperature probes and the pressure sensor are measuring, and that the data is being transmitted.
3. **Calibration**: calibrate the pressure sensor. See [`pressure sensors`](../../specs/v2-2025/sensors/differential%20pressure%20sensor/calibration/README_pressure_sensors.md).
4. **Seals**: check the condition of all seals (no cuts, no deformation, no dirt).
5. **Watertightness test**: this box must have passed a test of at least 6 h (see [04_tests.md](04_tests.md)).
6. **Closing**: close the electronics section with the drill, tightening the screws in a crisscross pattern. It is better to close the box indoors, in the dry, than in the field.
7. **Identification**: write down the box number and the start-up time on the deployment sheet.

## 3. Step-by-step installation

### Step 1: Survey and document the site

1. Take a **photo** of the site from the bank.
2. Write down the **GPS coordinates** on the sheet.
3. Measure the **water depth** at the chosen location.

### Step 2: Drive in the rod

1. Make a pilot hole in the riverbed with the **crowbar** and the **sledgehammer**.
2. Remove the crowbar.
3. Push the rod into the hole, **vertically**, with the probes clipped on. The rod is also connected to the box by a tube, so be careful with this tube.

### Step 3: Submerge the box

1. Lower the box into the water **gently**, without letting go of it.
2. Place it flat on the riverbed.
3. Check that:
   - the box is **fully submerged**;
   - it is **stable** and does not tip over in the current;
   - the probe cables are **neither taut nor kinked**.

### Step 4: Document the installation

1. Take a **photo** of the installation.
2. Write down the **time it went into the water** on the sheet.

## 4. Final checks

Before leaving the site:

| ✔ | Check |
|---|---|
| ☐ | Data is being received |
| ☐ | Temperature values are consistent (close to the water temperature) |
| ☐ | The pressure value is consistent |
| ☐ | The box is stable and fully submerged |
| ☐ | No cable is taut |
| ☐ | The deployment sheet is filled in |
| ☐ | All the equipment is packed back in the case |

---

## 5. During measurement

- Check regularly that data is arriving. **A sudden interruption may indicate a leak.**
- After a flood or a storm, go and check that the box is still in place.

---

## 6. Retrieval

1. Take the box out of the water **without pulling on the probe cables**.
2. Pull the rod out vertically, without twisting it.
3. **Dry the whole box before opening it**: a single drop falling inside can damage the electronics.
4. Open the box and **check the inside**: there must be no visible trace of moisture. If water got in, write it down on the sheet.
5. Remove the batteries.
6. Rinse the box, the rod and the seals with clean water, then let them dry.
7. Check the condition of the seals and probes. Replace anything damaged.
8. Pack everything back in the case and replace any missing items.

## 7. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| No data received | Flat batteries, out of range, flooded electronics | Check the batteries, move the receiver closer, open the box in a dry place and inspect it |
| Inconsistent temperature | Probe detached from the rod or damaged | Pull out the rod and check the clips |
| Inconsistent pressure | Blocked cavity (forgotten plug, sand, algae) | Remove the plugs, clean the cavity |
| Water in the box | Damaged or badly fitted seal, insufficient tightening | Replace the seal, retighten with the drill and redo a watertightness test (see [04_tests.md](04_tests.md)) |
| The rod won't go in | Riverbed too hard or too stony | Choose another spot, do not force it |
| The box moves with the current | Not enough ballast | Add weights to the holder |
